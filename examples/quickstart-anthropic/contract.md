# Consulting Agreement

Between **Northbridge Advisory Ltd** ("Northbridge") and **Lumen Retail plc** ("Lumen").
This agreement is fictional and was written for testing.

1. **Term.** The agreement runs for twelve months from 1 March 2026. It renews for further
   twelve-month periods unless either party gives written notice at least thirty days before
   the current term ends.
2. **Fees.** Lumen pays Northbridge a fixed fee of USD 90,000, invoiced in three equal
   instalments: on signing, after the second workshop, and after the final report.
3. **Payment.** Invoices are payable within 30 days of the invoice date. Late amounts carry
   interest of 1% per month.
4. **Expenses.** Travel that Lumen approves in advance is reimbursed at cost, up to USD 5,000
   per calendar quarter.
5. **Confidentiality.** Each party keeps the other's confidential information secret during the
   term and for three years after it ends.
6. **Materials.** Northbridge keeps ownership of methods and materials it created before or
   apart from this agreement. Once the fee is paid in full, Lumen receives a perpetual,
   non-exclusive licence to use the materials delivered under this agreement for its internal
   purposes only.
7. **Liability.** Each party's total liability is capped at the fees paid in the twelve months
   before the claim. Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential loss. The cap does
   not apply to fraud or wilful misconduct.
8. **Termination.** Either party may end the agreement for convenience on sixty days' written
   notice, or immediately by written notice if the other commits a material breach and does not
   remedy it within fifteen days of being told.
9. **Non-solicitation.** For twelve months after the agreement ends, neither party may solicit
   for employment any member of the other's staff who worked on the engagement.
10. **Law.** The agreement is governed by the laws of England and Wales, and the courts of London
    have exclusive jurisdiction.
